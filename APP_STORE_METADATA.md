## Description (4000 chars max)

Transform your real life into an RPG. Ascend turns your goals, habits, and daily disciplines into a gamified journey where every action builds your character.

**Build Your Life Character**
Choose from 12 life categories — Education, Career, Finance, Physical Fitness, Mental Health, Relationships, Discipline, Spiritual, Creativity, Leadership, Appearance, and Social. Each category has its own XP bar, level, and life rank. Watch your character evolve as you grow in real life.

**AI-Powered Quest Generator**
Type any goal — "I want to learn Arabic," "Get into medical school," "Build a startup" — and our AI creates a structured quest chain with step-by-step tasks, XP rewards, and an inspirational narrative. Your ambitions become achievable missions.

**Track What Matters**
- Daily habits with streak tracking and flame counters
- Discipline practices with frequency scheduling  
- Active goals broken into completable tasks
- Life Map showing all 12 categories at a glance

**Premium Life Dashboard**
The signature dashboard shows your momentum score, today's mission with cinematic imagery, category progress rings, and an orbital Life Map — all in a dark luxury design inspired by the best productivity apps.

**Philosophy**
Ascend is built on Jim Rohn's philosophy: your life doesn't get better by chance, it gets better by change. Every small action compounds into transformation. The app tracks those actions and reflects your growth back to you as progress, levels, and identity evolution.

"Your life is worth designing." — Jim Rohn
