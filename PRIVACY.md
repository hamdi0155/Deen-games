# Privacy Policy — Ascend

Last updated: May 2026

Ascend stores all user data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information.

**Data stored locally:**
- Your character name and avatar selection
- Habit and discipline progress, streaks, and completion history
- Goal and quest data
- XP and level progress

**AI Quest Generation:**
When you use the AI Quest Generator, your goal description and selected category are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to generate quest content. This data is processed per Anthropic's privacy policy (anthropic.com/privacy) and is not stored by Ascend.

**No accounts required.** No sign-up. No data leaves your device except for AI quest generation.
